MisteryFriend Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026. This applies to the MisteryFriend Android app and to this website.

What it is

MisteryFriend organizes a Secret Santa: the organizer creates a session, each participant joins, and a draw decides who gives a gift to whom. Each participant receives their result by email.

What data is collected

  • For each participant: the name they enter, their email address and, if they want, a gift idea and a note for whoever gives to them.
  • For each session: its name, budget, date and rules, as entered by the organizer.

No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or device data are collected. There are no ads and no analytics tools. The app only asks for the Internet permission.

What it is used for

  • The email address: to send a 4-digit code that checks the address belongs to the person joining, and to send the result of the draw.
  • The name: so the organizer knows who has joined, and to tell each person who they are giving to.
  • The gift idea and the note: so that whoever gives to you can see them, after the draw.

They are not used for anything else, they are not sold and they are not shared with anyone.

Who sees what

  • The organizer sees the names and whether each person has confirmed their email. Email addresses appear hidden to them (for example, a•••@g•••.com).
  • Nobody sees who got whom; each participant only sees their own match. The organizer can unlock the full draw results, for example to help someone who has lost theirs.
  • Your gift idea and your note are only seen by the person who is giving to you.
  • Nobody can use MisteryFriend to find out whether an email address has joined.

Where it is stored and for how long

On a Google service (Apps Script and Google Drive) in the account of the app's author, who acts as data processor. Data travels encrypted (https).

Each session is deleted completely after 90 days without changes, or sooner if the organizer deletes it from the app. Before the draw, each participant can remove themselves from the app or from this website. Emails already sent stay in the recipient's inbox.

On the phone, the app only stores the code of each session and the keys to get back in. They are not included in the phone's backups.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to jarvis.cordoba.ia@gmail.com. We will reply within one month at most. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

Children

MisteryFriend is not intended for children under 14. If a child takes part in a family Secret Santa, an adult should sign them up with their own email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its new date.
